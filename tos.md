## 1. Description of Service

Twilight Garden is a cycle tracking and wellness application designed for individuals and couples. The App provides the following features:

### Core Features
- **Period & cycle tracking** — Log menstrual flow, predict upcoming periods, and track cycle history
- **Daily health logging** — Record moods, physical symptoms, digestive symptoms, energy levels, sleep quality, and personal notes
- **Cycle predictions** — Automated predictions for period start, fertile window, and ovulation based on your logged data
- **Calendar view** — Visual calendar with cycle phase indicators and logged data

### Partner Features (Couples Mode)
- **Partner pairing** — Link your account with a partner using a pairing code
- **Shared cycle insights** — Your partner can view your cycle data and daily logs (with your consent)
- **Love Notes** — End-to-end encrypted chat with text, images, voice messages, and GIFs
- **Audio & video calls** — Peer-to-peer WebRTC calling between partners
- **Interactive games** — 19 couple games including trivia, word games, and classic board games
- **Ghost Mode** — Temporarily hide your cycle data from your partner while maintaining chat access

### AI-Powered Features
- **Wellness tips** — AI-generated health and wellness suggestions based on your cycle phase and symptoms
- **Empathy alerts** — AI-generated guidance for partners to better support their menstruating partner
- **Love note suggestions** — AI-generated romantic messages
- **Gift recommendations** — AI-suggested products based on your partner's current wellness needs

### Additional Features
- **Push notifications & reminders** — Configurable daily logging reminders and period predictions
- **PDF health reports** — Export your cycle and health data as a downloadable PDF
- **Shared insight cards** — Generate shareable wellness insight cards with expiring links
- **Multi-device support** — Use the App on Android, desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux), and web browsers
- **End-to-end encryption** — Military-grade encryption for chat messages, media, and health data payloads
- **Home screen widgets** — Android widgets for quick access to partner status and quick actions

- You may link your account with one partner at a time using a 6-character pairing code.

- Partners are assigned roles: "menstruator" or "supporter."

### What Is Shared
By enabling Couples Mode, you explicitly consent to sharing the following with your linked partner:
- Your profile information (name, avatar, bio, status)

### Your Controls
